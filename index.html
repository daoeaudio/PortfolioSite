Bio

Hi, I'm Daniel, a game sound designer based in Stockholm, Sweden. Thanks for visiting my portfolio! Currently, I'm a Senior Sound Designer at Star Stable Entertainment, where I've spent the last five years shaping the audio of Star Stable Online. Working on a long running live game has taught me how to balance innovation while respecting the legacy, solve creative and technical challenges, and deliver high-quality work, even under tight deadlines. In my spare time, I enjoy field recording, playing video games and running. Feel free to check out my resume here! ps. I have also uploaded some of my field recordings on this site that you are welcome to download and use for your projects, except for training ai.

Star Stable Online

Dynamic Hoof Sound System

I reworked the hoof sound system for horses in Star Stable Online. I recorded real horses, created new assets and implemented a flexible system that works across 80+ horse animation rigs. The sounds adapt dynamically based on material, speed, which leg, weight, and tack. This was a cross-disciplinary effort involving close collaboration with tech animators and programmers.

Ambience for Hollow Woods

This video showcases the ambience I created for the Hollow Woods area in Star Stable Online. The ambience will change depending on the time. The area also includes distinct reverb zones to enhance immersion and in this case create a magical feeling to the area.
